## English

Apex Fit estimates your body fat percentage, daily calorie target, macros and a weekly workout plan from your height, weight, age and sex.

**Free**
- Body fat estimate, BMI, fat mass and lean mass
- Daily calories (BMR and TDEE) for fat loss, maintenance or muscle gain
- Protein, carbs and fat split
- General weekly workout plan

**PRO**
- Detailed daily meal plan with calories and macros per meal
- Workout days with exercises, sets and reps
- Progress tracker (weight and waist) with a chart

> Estimates only, based on general formulas. Not medical advice.

## بالعربي

**Apex Fit** برنامج بيحسب لك نسبة الدهون التقريبية والسعرات اليومية والماكروز وخطة تمارين أسبوعية، من الطول والوزن والسن والنوع.
